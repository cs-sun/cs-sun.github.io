Research Interests

  • Agentic AI safety — long-horizon agent evaluation, behavioral drift, multi-agent systems
  • Adversarial ML — jailbreaks, adversarial robustness, risk-aware robust optimization
  • Interpretability & reliability — GNN explainability, runtime failure detection, input repair

Other Expertise

  • LLM post-training — supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning
  • AI systems — model development, deployment monitoring, and large-scale data pipelines (Spark, ClickHouse)
  • Distributed training — multi-GPU training and distributed reinforcement learning with Ray
